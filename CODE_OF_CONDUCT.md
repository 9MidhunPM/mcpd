# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid abusive, discriminatory, or threatening behavior.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner; do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This compact policy may be replaced by a formally adopted community standard as the contributor community grows.
